What is Rick and Morty?

Rick and Morty is an critically acclaimed adult animated science-fiction sitcom created by Justin Roiland and Dan Harmon. The series follows Rick Sanchez—an eccentric, nihilistic genius scientist—and his good-hearted but easily distressed grandson Morty Smith as they split their lives between domestic family drama and dangerous interdimensional adventures across infinite realities.

Armed with Rick's portal gun and flying saucer, the duo encounters bizarre alien civilizations, alternate timelines, clones, and the bureaucratic Citadel of Ricks. Use this interactive multiverse database to inspect every documented specimen from the show.
